## Decisions Made
- **viem explicit install:** pnpm's strict hoisting doesn't expose peer deps — viem installed explicitly as direct dependency
- **wagmi v2 pinned:** RainbowKit 2.2.10 peer depends on wagmi ^2.9.0; wagmi v3 (3.5.0) is NOT compatible
- **Dynamic session TTL:** Computed from SIWE expirationTime with `Math.min(computed, MAX_SESSION_TTL_S)` and `DEFAULT_SESSION_TTL_S` fallback when computed ≤ 0
- **No "use client" on web3 modules:** config.ts and client.ts are pure config modules importable by both client and server

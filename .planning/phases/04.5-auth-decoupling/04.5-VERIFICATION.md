## Summary

All 9 must-haves are verified. The phase successfully replaces Proxmox-coupled authentication with RainbowKit + SIWE, restricted to Universal Profiles on LUKSO. The session model stores signature, message, and messageHash in Redis with dynamic TTL derived from the SIWE message expiration time.
